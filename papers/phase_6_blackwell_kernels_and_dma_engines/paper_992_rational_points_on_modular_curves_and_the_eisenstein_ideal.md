## Abstract
We investigate the geometry of the modular curve $X_0(p)$ and its Jacobian variety $J_0(p)$ under the action of the Hecke algebra $\mathbb{T}$. Barry Mazur's profound insight was that the Eisenstein ideal $I \subset \mathbb{T}$, generated by $T_\ell - (\ell + 1)$ for primes $\ell \neq p$ and $1 + w_p$, controls the cuspidal divisor class group and yields an optimal quotient $J_0(p) \to A$ whose rational points $A(\mathbb{Q})$ are entirely finite torsion.

### 1. The Eisenstein Quotient
The Eisenstein quotient $J_e = J_0(p) / I J_0(p)$ has dimension $1$ when $p = 11$, and in general its $\mathbb{Q}$-rational points satisfy:
$$J_e(\mathbb{Q}) \cong \mathbb{Z} / m \mathbb{Z}, \quad m = \text{numerator}\left(\frac{p-1}{12}\right)$$
This finite quotient directly annihilates any potential rational points on $Y_0(p)(\mathbb{Q})$ for $p \ge 11$, precluding points of high prime order.
