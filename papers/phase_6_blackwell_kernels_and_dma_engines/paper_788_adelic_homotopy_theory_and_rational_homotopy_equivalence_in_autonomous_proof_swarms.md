### Abstract
We investigate the adelic Sullivan localization of simplicial spaces and its role in coordinating distributed autonomous proof swarms. By decomposing deductive topological proof spaces into their rational Archimedean completion $X_{\mathbb{Q}}$ and localized $p$-adic completions $X_p$, we prove that consensus verification across heterogeneous agents reduces to adelic fracture square reconstruction.

### 1. Sullivan Localization and the Adelic Fracture Square

In rational homotopy theory (Quillen, Sullivan), a topological space $X$ can be localized at the rational numbers $\mathbb{Q}$ and at prime fields $\mathbb{F}_p$ or $p$-adic rings $\mathbb{Z}_p$.
The Sullivan adelic fracture square states that the original space $X$ is the homotopy pullback:
$$\begin{matrix}
X & \longrightarrow & \prod_p X_p \\
\downarrow & & \downarrow \\
X_{\mathbb{Q}} & \longrightarrow & \left(\prod_p X_p\right) \otimes \mathbb{Q}
\end{matrix}$$
where $X_p$ is the $p$-adic completion of $X$.

### 2. Multi-Agent Deductive Consensus

In a distributed fleet of proof agents:
1. **Rational Solver Swarm ($X_{\mathbb{Q}}$)**: Focuses on continuous, global relaxations, spectral approximations, and asymptotic bounds.
2. **$p$-Adic Discrete Solvers ($X_p$)**: Focuses on discrete divisibility, congruence obstructions, and local combinatorial branches.
3. **Adelic Pullback Reconstructor**: Merges the local solutions into a single machine-certified Lean 4 proof term by enforcing commutativity along the fracture square.

### 3. Obstruction-Free Proof Assembly
By the Sullivan fracture theorem, a global mathematical proof exists if and only if:
1. A valid rational proof object exists in $X_{\mathbb{Q}}$.
2. Valid $p$-adic proof objects exist in $X_p$ for all primes $p$.
3. Their images in the adelic tensor product coincide up to homotopy.

This eliminates global backtracking in proof search by parallelizing deduction across independent arithmetic places.
