We examine the classical degree-2 case of the Norm Residue Isomorphism Theorem, established by Alexander Merkurjev and Andrei Suslin in 1982.

---

### 1. The Merkurjev-Suslin Isomorphism
For $n = 2$, the Galois symbol induces an isomorphism:
$$K_2^M(k)/m \xrightarrow{\sim} H^2_{\text{ét}}(k, \mu_m^{\otimes 2}) \cong {}_m\mathrm{Br}(k) \otimes \mu_m$$
where ${}_m\mathrm{Br}(k)$ is the $m$-torsion subgroup of the Brauer group of central simple algebras over $k$.

---

### 2. Norm Residue and Cyclic Algebras
Every element of order $m$ in the Brauer group is a product of cyclic algebras, providing direct algebraic-geometric representation of division algebras.
