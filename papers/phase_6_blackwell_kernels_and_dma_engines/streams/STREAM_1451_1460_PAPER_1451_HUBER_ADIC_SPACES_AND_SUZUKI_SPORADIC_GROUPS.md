## 2. Mathematical Formalization: Huber Adic Spaces in Lean 4

Let $(A, A^+)$ be a Huber pair consisting of a Huber ring $A$ and an open, integrally closed subring $A^+ \subseteq A^\circ$.
The fundamental theorems established by Huber state:
1. **Spectral Topology**:
   $X = \mathrm{Spa}(A, A^+)$ is a spectral topological space: sober, quasi-compact, and possessing an open basis of quasi-compact subsets stable under finite intersections.
2. **Rational Open Neighborhoods**:
   Subsets of the form $U(T/s) = \{ v \in \mathrm{Spa}(A, A^+) \mid v(t) \le v(s) \neq 0, \forall t \in T \}$ form a basis of the topology.
3. **Structure Sheaf & Sheafiness**:
   For any Tate ring $A$ that is strongly noetherian (or perfectoid), the presheaf $\mathcal{O}_X$ defined by rational localizations $\mathcal{O}_X(U(T/s)) = A\langle T/s \rangle$ is a sheaf of complete topological rings.
4. **Higher-Rank Specializations**:
   The closure of a point $v \in \mathrm{Spa}(A, A^+)$ consists of valuations horizontal or vertical to $v$, capturing boundaries of rigid analytic varieties as boundary valuation fibers.
