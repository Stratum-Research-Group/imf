Let $\mathrm{ProFin}$ be the category of profinite sets. A condensed set is a sheaf of sets on $\mathrm{ProFin}$ equipped with the topology generated by finite joint surjective families. Clausen and Scholze establish:

1. **Abelian Category Structure**:
   $\mathrm{Cond}(\mathrm{Ab})$ is a Grothendieck abelian category satisfying AB5 (exact filtered colimits) and AB3* (all limits exist).
2. **Solid Modules $\mathrm{Solid}(\mathbb{Z})$**:
   The full subcategory of solid abelian groups is reflective: the inclusion functor admits a left adjoint $(-)\otimes^\blacksquare \mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare$.
   For any profinite set $S = \varprojlim S_i$, the solid completion $\mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare[S] = \varprojlim \mathbb{Z}[S_i]$ is complete and nuclear.
3. **Symmetric Solid Tensor**:
   The solid tensor product $\otimes^\blacksquare$ is symmetric, associative, and closed monoidal.
4. **Projective Covers by Extremally Disconnected Sets**:
   Every compact Hausdorff space admits a surjection from an extremally disconnected set (Stone-Čech compactification $\beta D$).
