## 2. Mathematical Formalization: Fontaine Period Rings in Lean 4

Let $C$ be an algebraically closed complete extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, and let $C^\flat$ be its tilt. Let $A_{\inf} = W(\mathcal{O}_{C^\flat})$, and $\theta : A_{\inf} \to \mathcal{O}_C$ be Fontaine's map.
The foundational theorems established by Fontaine state:
1. **Infinitesimal Kernel Principal**:
   $\ker(\theta)$ is a principal ideal generated by an element $\xi = [\pi^\flat] + p \in A_{\inf}$.
2. **Discrete Valuation Ring Structure**:
   $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ = \varprojlim_k A_{\inf}[1/p] / (\ker \theta)^k$ is a complete discrete valuation ring with uniformizer $\xi$ and residue field $C$.
3. **Filtered Field $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$**:
   $B_{\mathrm{dR}} = \mathrm{Frac}(B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+) = B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+[1/\xi]$ possesses an exhaustive, separated decreasing filtration $\mathrm{Fil}^i B_{\mathrm{dR}} = \xi^i B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$.
4. **Crystalline Invariants**:
   The crystalline period ring $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$ satisfies $B_{\mathrm{cris}}^{\varphi = 1} \cap \mathrm{Fil}^0 B_{\mathrm{cris}} = \mathbb{Q}_p$.
5. **de Rham Comparison Isomorphism**:
   For any smooth projective variety $X/K$, $H_{\acute{e}t}^m(X_{\overline{K}}, \mathbb{Q}_p) \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}_p} B_{\mathrm{dR}} \cong H_{\mathrm{dR}}^m(X/K) \otimes_K B_{\mathrm{dR}}$ is an isomorphism of filtered $G_K$-modules.
