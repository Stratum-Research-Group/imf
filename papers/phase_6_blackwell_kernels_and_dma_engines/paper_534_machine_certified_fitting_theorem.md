### 1. Theorem Statement and Algebraic Invariants
Let $G$ be a group, and let $M \trianglelefteq G$ and $N \trianglelefteq G$ be normal nilpotent subgroups with lower central series terminating at lengths $c$ and $d$ respectively:
$$\gamma_{c+1}(M) = 1, \quad \gamma_{d+1}(N) = 1.$$
Fitting's theorem (1938) asserts that:
1. The product $MN$ is a normal subgroup of $G$.
2. $MN$ is nilpotent.
3. The nilpotency class of $MN$ satisfies $\text{nil}(MN) \le c + d$.
