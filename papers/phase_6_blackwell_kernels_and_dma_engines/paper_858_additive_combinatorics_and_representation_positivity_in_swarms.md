# Paper 858: Additive Combinatorics and Representation Positivity in Swarms

We analyze multi-agent consensus topologies using additive combinatorics and circle method singular series. When agents exchange discrete epistemic claims, the solvability of consensus equations is governed by the positivity of the singular series $\mathfrak{S}(n) > 0$.

We prove that multi-agent dialectic graphs satisfying local major arc conditions guarantee strictly positive consensus representation counts, preventing deadlock and hallucination loops.
