# Paper 815: Interactive WebGL 3D Archimedean Fiber Raytracing and Kähler Geodesics

We detail the mathematical graphics engine implemented in `docs/assets/arakelov_surface_manifold_3d.html`. The interactive renderer projects the arithmetic fibration $\mathcal{X} \to \mathrm{Spec}(\mathcal{O}_K)$ over finite primes alongside the continuous complex archimedean fiber equipped with Kähler metrics and Green's current waveforms.
