### Abstract
We examine the structure of Selmer groups $\mathrm{Sel}_r(E/\mathbb{Q})$ and Tate-Shafarevich groups $\Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})$ within multi-agent deductive verification swarms. By modeling inter-agent dialectic consensus as a short exact sequence of Galois cohomology modules $0 	o E(\mathbb{Q})/r E(\mathbb{Q}) 	o \mathrm{Sel}_r(E/\mathbb{Q}) 	o \Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})[r] 	o 0$, we provide a rigorous mathematical bound on dialectic conflict resolution times.
