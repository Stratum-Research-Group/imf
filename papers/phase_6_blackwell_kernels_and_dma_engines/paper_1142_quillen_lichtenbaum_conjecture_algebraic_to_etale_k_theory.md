We examine the mathematical formulation and proof of the Quillen-Lichtenbaum Conjecture for number fields and rings of integers.

---

### 1. The Quillen Comparison Map
Let $F$ be a number field and $\mathcal{O}_F$ its ring of integers. For all degrees $n \ge 2$, Daniel Quillen and Stephen Lichtenbaum conjectured that:
$$K_n(\mathcal{O}_F) \to K_n^{\text{ét}}(\mathcal{O}_F)$$
is an isomorphism up to finite 2-torsion.

---

### 2. Resolution via Motivic Cohomology
The conjecture is resolved by expressing both algebraic and étale K-theory as target groups of the motivic spectral sequence, where the Beilinson-Lichtenbaum isomorphism ensures isomorphism in the stable range.
