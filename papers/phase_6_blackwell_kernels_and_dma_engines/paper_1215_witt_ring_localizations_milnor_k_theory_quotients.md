## Abstract
We investigate the fibered product decomposition of Milnor-Witt $K$-theory over the powers of the fundamental ideal $I \subset \mathbf{GW}(k)$. By examining the exact sequences relating $\mathbf{K}^{MW}_n(k)$, $\mathbf{K}^M_n(k)$, and $I^n(k)$, we prove that Milnor-Witt $K$-theory forms the exact universal fiber product:
$$\mathbf{K}^{MW}_n(k) \cong \mathbf{K}^M_n(k) \times_{I^n / I^{n+1}} I^n(k)$$
linking Milnor's conjecture to the stable stems of motivic homotopy theory.
