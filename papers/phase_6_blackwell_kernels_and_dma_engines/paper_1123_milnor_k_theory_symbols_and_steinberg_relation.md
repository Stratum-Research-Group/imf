We analyze the graded ring structure of Milnor K-theory $K_*^M(k)$ and the algebraic necessity of the Steinberg relation.

---

### 1. Graded Ring Definition
Milnor K-theory is defined as the quotient of the tensor algebra of the multiplicative group $k^\times$:
$$K_*^M(k) = T(k^\times) / \mathcal{I}$$
where $\mathcal{I}$ is the two-sided ideal generated by elements of the form $a \otimes (1 - a)$ for $a \in k^\times \setminus \{1\}$.

---

### 2. Steinberg Vanishing & Symbol Products
The fundamental relation $\{a, 1 - a\} = 0$ ensures anti-commutativity:
$$\{a, b\} = -\{b, a\}$$
and enforces quadratic reciprocity in arithmetic fields.
