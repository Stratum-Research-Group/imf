## Abstract
This treatise provides a rigorous mathematical exposition of Milnor-Witt $K$-theory $\mathbf{K}^{MW}_*(k)$. We analyze its presentation by generators $[u]$ for $u \in k^\times$ and the Hopf element $\eta$, subject to the Steinberg relations, the commutativity relations, and the hyperbolic relation $\eta h = 0$ where $h = \langle 1 \rangle + \langle -1 \rangle$.

## 1. Generators and Relations of K^MW
Let $k$ be a field. The graded ring $\mathbf{K}^{MW}_*(k)$ is the associative graded ring with generators:
* $[u]$ in degree $+1$, for each $u \in k^\times$
* $\eta$ in degree $-1$
satisfying:
1. $[u][1 - u] = 0$ for all $u \in k^\times \setminus \{1\}$
2. $[uv] = [u] + [v] + \eta [u][v]$
3. $[u]\eta = \eta[u]$
4. $\eta h = 0$, where $h = 2 + \eta[-1] = \langle 1 \rangle + \langle -1 \rangle$.

## 2. Comparison with Milnor K-Theory and Witt Rings
Taking the quotient by the two-sided ideal generated by $\eta$ yields an isomorphism with Milnor $K$-theory:
$$\mathbf{K}^{MW}_*(k) / (\eta) \overset{\cong}{\longrightarrow} \mathbf{K}^M_*(k)$$
Inverting $\eta$ yields an isomorphism with the localized Witt ring:
$$\mathbf{K}^{MW}_*(k)[\eta^{-1}] \overset{\cong}{\longrightarrow} W(k)[\eta^{\pm 1}]$$
