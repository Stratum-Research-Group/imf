We examine the behavior of motivic complexes and weight filtrations over finite fields $\mathbb{F}_q$.

---

### 1. Weight Degeneracy over Finite Fields
For smooth varieties over finite fields, the Frobenius automorphism acts semi-simplistically, and higher Chow groups $\mathrm{CH}^q(X, 2q - p)$ vanish outside specific arithmetic intervals.

---

### 2. Special Values of Zeta Functions
The orders and leading coefficients of Hasse-Weil zeta functions at integer arguments are directly expressed in terms of the Euler characteristics of motivic cohomology complexes.
