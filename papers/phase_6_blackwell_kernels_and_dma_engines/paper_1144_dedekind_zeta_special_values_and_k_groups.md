### 1. The Lichtenbaum Conjectures
Lichtenbaum conjectured that for totally real number fields $F$ and $k \ge 1$:
$$\zeta_F(1 - 2k) = \pm \frac{|K_{4k-2}(\mathcal{O}_F)|}{|K_{4k-1}(\mathcal{O}_F)|} \cdot 2^{r_1(F)}$$
linking the orders of finite algebraic K-groups to analytic L-functions.
