### 1. Completely Decomposed Topology
The Nisnevich topology is strictly finer than the Zariski topology and coarser than the étale topology. It satisfies descent for algebraic K-theory and higher Chow complexes without requiring completion.

---

### 2. The Étale Gap
The discrepancy between Nisnevich motivic cohomology and étale cohomology begins precisely above the diagonal $p > q$, governed by the canonical truncation boundary.
