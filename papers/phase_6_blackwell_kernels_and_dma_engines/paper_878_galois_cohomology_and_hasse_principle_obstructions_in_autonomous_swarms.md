We model multi-agent consensus failures as Hasse principle obstructions in Galois cohomology. Local consensus across individual agent subsets (local places $v$) does not automatically guarantee global consensus (global rational point) when the Shafarevich-Tate obstruction group is non-trivial. We formulate an obstruction-free consensus algorithm with machine-certified convergence guarantees.
