## 2. Mathematical Foundations

### 2.1 The Harry Dym Evolution Equation
The Harry Dym equation governs a strictly positive field $u(x, t) > 0$:
$$\partial_t u = u^3 \partial_x^3 u$$
The associated non-standard Lax operator is:
$$L = -u^2 \partial_x^2$$

$$\mathcal{D}_1 = u^3 \partial_x^3 \circ u^3$$
with Poisson-commuting Casimirs $\mathcal{C}_0 = \int u^{-1} dx$ and Hamiltonians $\mathcal{H}_1 = \frac{1}{2} \int u_x^2 dx$.

$$y = \int^x u(x', t) \, dx', \quad \tau = t, \quad v(y, \tau) = u(x, t)$$
